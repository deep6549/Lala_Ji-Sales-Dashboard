# Lala_Ji-Sales-Dashboard
# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Power BI business intelligence dashboard** designed to analyze e-commerce sales performance across customers, products, locations, payment methods, and time periods. The dashboard transforms raw order data into clear visual insights, helping users understand **sales trends, profitability, product performance, customer contribution, and regional performance**.

---

## 1. Project Title / Headline

### 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard

A dynamic and interactive **Power BI dashboard** built to explore e-commerce sales data, analyze business performance, and identify trends across **sales amount, profit, quantity, customers, product sub-categories, payment modes, and geographical regions**.

---

## 2. Short Description / Purpose

The E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard provides an interactive view of business performance using historical order and sales data. It enables users to monitor key KPIs, identify profitable product segments, analyze monthly sales trends, compare state-wise performance, and understand customer and payment-mode patterns through interactive visualizations and filters.

The dashboard is designed to support **business analysis, performance monitoring, and data-driven decision-making**.

---

## 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was developed using the following tools and technologies:

* 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Main platform used to build the interactive dashboard and visualizations.
* 📂 **Power Query** – Used for importing, cleaning, transforming, and preparing the raw order data.
* 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Used to create calculated measures such as **Amount, Profit, Quantity, and Average Order Value (AOV)**.
* 🗂️ **Data Modeling** – Used to connect and analyze order, customer, product, and sales-related information.
* 📅 **Date Hierarchy** – Used for analyzing sales across months and quarters.
* 📈 **Data Visualization** – Bar charts, column charts, donut charts, KPI cards, and slicers were used to present business insights interactively.
* 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` / `.pbit` for Power BI development and dashboard sharing.

---

## 4. Data Source

The dashboard uses structured **e-commerce order and sales data** containing information about:

* Order ID and Order Date
* Customer names
* State and City
* Product categories and sub-categories
* Sales amount
* Profit
* Quantity
* Payment mode
* Average Order Value

The data was cleaned and prepared before being used for visualization and analysis in Power BI.

# 5. Features / Highlights

## 🔹 Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate large amounts of transactional data, but raw order records can make it difficult to quickly understand overall business performance.

Important questions such as:

* Which states generate the highest sales?
* Which product sub-categories generate the most profit?
* How does sales performance change over time?
* Which customers contribute the most revenue?
* Which payment methods are used most frequently?
* Which product categories account for the highest quantity sold?

can be difficult to answer efficiently from raw data.

### 🎯 Goal of the Dashboard

The goal of this dashboard is to convert raw e-commerce data into an **interactive business intelligence solution** that allows users to:

* Monitor overall sales and profitability.
* Track important business KPIs.
* Identify monthly sales trends.
* Compare regional sales performance.
* Analyze product and sub-category profitability.
* Understand customer-level sales contribution.
* Analyze payment-mode usage.
* Filter and explore the data dynamically by **city and quarter**.

---

## 🔹 Walkthrough of Key Visuals

### 📌 KPI Cards

The dashboard displays key performance indicators at the top, including:

* **Total Sales Amount**
* **Total Profit**
* **Total Quantity Sold**
* **Average Order Value (AOV)**

These KPIs provide an immediate overview of overall business performance.

### 📈 Monthly Sales Trend

A column chart shows **sales amount across different months**, allowing users to identify periods of higher and lower sales and understand the overall sales trend over time.

### 🗺️ State-wise Sales Analysis

A bar chart compares **sales amount across different states**, helping identify the strongest-performing regions and differences in geographical sales performance.

### 👥 Customer Sales Analysis

The dashboard includes a customer-level sales visualization that compares the **sales contribution of individual customers**, helping identify high-value customers.

### 💰 Profit by Sub-Category

A bar chart analyzes **profit across product sub-categories**, making it possible to identify the most profitable and least profitable product segments.

### 📦 Quantity by Category

A donut chart displays the **quantity sold across different product categories**, providing a quick view of product demand and category contribution.

### 💳 Payment Mode Analysis

A donut chart visualizes **quantity by payment mode**, allowing users to understand customer payment preferences.

### 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive slicers for:

* **City**
* **Quarter**

These filters allow users to dynamically explore the dashboard and analyze performance for specific locations and time periods.

## 🔹 Business Impact & Insights

### 📊 Performance Monitoring

The KPI section provides a quick overview of sales, profit, quantity, and AOV, making it easier to monitor business performance.

### 💰 Profitability Analysis

Sub-category-level profit analysis helps identify which products contribute most strongly to overall profitability.

### 🌎 Regional Analysis

State-wise sales analysis allows businesses to identify high-performing regions and potential areas for further growth.

### 👥 Customer Analysis

Customer-level sales data can help identify high-value customers and understand their contribution to total revenue.

### 📦 Product Analysis

Category and sub-category analysis provides insights into product demand and profitability.

### 💳 Payment Behavior

Payment-mode analysis helps understand how customers prefer to complete their purchases.

### 📅 Time-Based Analysis

Monthly and quarterly analysis makes it easier to identify sales patterns and changes in business performance over time.
